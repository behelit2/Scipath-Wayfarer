Scipath Wayfarer 🌍 · Player's Guide

Instruction Manual

Welcome, Wayfarer! The old world is gone, but humankind's story is not over. Keep this field guide close as you cross the wilderness, challenge the ten regional bosses, rebuild the villages, and take to the sky!

A complete guide to adventure after the old world

1. The Story

Three hundred and fifty years ago, a terrible nuclear catastrophe destroyed the old civilization. Its great cities fell, its machines were abandoned, and strange creatures spread across the wilderness.

The people who remain live in small villages separated by dangerous country. Ten powerful regional bosses now keep the world divided. You are a Wayfarer: a traveler willing to cross the wilds, help the settlements, and face the powers that threaten them.

Your mission: break the rule of all ten regional bosses!

2. Object of the Game

Defeat the boss in each of the ten regions. When all ten bosses have fallen, the main campaign is won!
  1. Explore the country around you.
  2. Fight monsters and earn experience, gold, and levels.
  3. Visit villages, rest at inns, and improve your equipment.
  4. Ask residents what they know about the regional boss.
  5. Follow the report to the boss's lair and defeat it.
  6. Travel outward and repeat the journey in all ten regions.

You may fight the bosses in any order, but the outer regions are much more dangerous. Rebuilding villages, finding discoveries, operating radio towers, establishing trade, responding to settlement crises, and building airships are optional adventures.

3. Starting a Game

  • Enter your hero's name.
  • Choose Easy – Wanderer, Normal – Adventurer, or Hard – Wayfarer.
  • Select Start New Game.

Easy gives you a kinder beginning, better rewards, and lighter penalties. Normal is the balanced adventure. Hard has stronger enemies, scarcer money, slower growth, and harsher defeat penalties.

Scipath Wayfarer has one browser save slot. Starting a new game while a save exists will ask before replacing it.

Read the Status Display

  • Hero Lv is your character level.
  • Region Lv shows the strength of the surrounding region, from 1 to 10.
  • On Foot, Boat, Airship shows your current travel mode.
  • HP is your health. At zero HP, you are defeated.
  • XP shows progress toward your next level.
  • Gold pays for inns, shops, boats, and rebuilding.
  • Provisions are used at sea and in the air.
  • Bosses shows how many regional bosses have fallen.

5. Reading the Map

The world is made of square tiles. Land color tells you the biome, while special pictures mark villages, ruins, towers, discoveries, boss lairs, and airships.

Land and Sea

Land biomes have different creatures, but the Region Level is the best warning of overall danger. The farther you travel from the starting country, the higher the region level becomes.

Travelers and Landmarks

6. Traveling the World

Every step moves you one tile. On land, a wandering monster may attack. Defeating the boss of a region permanently reduces random encounters in that region by half.

Region levels rise as you travel outward. You may enter a dangerous region early, but its creatures, rewards, shops, and boss are meant for a stronger hero.

Travel also advances the lives of settlements you have discovered. Villages produce necessities, exchange limited aid, respond to regional danger, and remember major changes while you are away.

Crossing the Sea

  • Entering the sea on foot rents a boat for 10 gold.
  • Every water tile uses one provision.
  • Landing leaves the boat behind.
  • Your next sea departure requires another boat rental.
  • Enemy encounters do not occur while boating.
  • If provisions reach zero, further sea movement removes HP and can defeat you.
Count provisions for both the outward voyage and the return. Item Shops sell packs of 200 travel provisions.

Flying an Airship

  • An airship moves twice as fast as ordinary travel.
  • One provision supports two air tiles.
  • Enemy encounters do not occur in the air.
  • You may fly over water, wilderness, and landmarks.
  • Flying over a village or landmark does not activate it. Land nearby and approach on foot.
  • The airship can land only on an empty grass, beach, or desert tile.
  • Towns, ruins, towers, discoveries, lairs, and other airships block landing.

During flight, the airship's engine sound repeats one time after another. It has no sea-travel song. Active radio broadcasts can still be heard exactly as they are on land.

When provisions are gone, every further air tile removes HP. If you are defeated in the air, the airship you are flying is destroyed and you return to your refuge.

7. Villages

Step onto a village tile to enter. A newly visited settlement receives a name and is remembered on your map.

Village Notes and Conditions

Open Village Notes to review the settlement's present condition and important developments since your previous visit. Residents also discuss local changes in their own words.

Every known village has four living conditions:

  • Supplies represent food, medicine, ordinary necessities, and communal travel stores.
  • Materials represent tools, wood, metal, salvage, and repair stock.
  • Security represents local defenses, route safety, and resistance to raids.
  • Prosperity represents economic health, productive capacity, and confidence.

Conditions may be abundant, stable, strained, or dire. A village's biome, shops, regional boss, nearby settlements, reconstruction links, patrols, confederation, and trade agreements all influence how it fares.

A dire condition does not erase a village, close its shops, or remove its services. It may create an emergency that neighboring settlements or the Wayfarer can help resolve.

Residents

Speak with the villagers. They offer advice, remember changes in their settlement, report shortages and recoveries, and may know where the regional boss lives. If one resident does not know, ask another.

Different residents notice different concerns. Farmers often speak about supplies, guards about security, traders about prosperity and exchange, and mechanists about tools and repair materials.

The Inn

An inn stay restores all HP and registers that village as your recovery refuge. If you are later defeated, you return to that refuge with full health. The price is shown on the inn button.

Simply entering a village does not heal you. You must choose Stay at the Inn, even when the stay is free.

Shops

  • Weapon Shops sell equipment that raises attack.
  • Armor Shops sell equipment that raises defense.
  • Item Shops sell healing supplies, battle items, wards, smoke, and travel provisions.

Not every village has every shop. Outer-region shops carry stronger equipment, but their prices are higher.

Rebuild and Trade

Reconstruction and confederation trade become available after the boss of that village's region has been defeated.

Airship Assembly is different. Its workshop appears in the Rebuild tab of every village in Regions 6 through 10, even when the ordinary Reconstruction Board is still locked.

8. Battle

When a monster appears, choose an action:

  • Attack - strike with your equipped weapon.
  • Inventory - use an item or change equipment. This uses your battle action.
  • Flee - try to escape. A failed attempt lets the enemy answer.

Ordinary enemies and mini bosses may be fled. A regional boss seals its lair and cannot be fled.

Watch the Gold Warning Box

Bosses and mini bosses show their next response before you act. They may prepare a heavy blow, raise their armor, heal, pierce your defense, or strike more than once. Read the warning and choose your action carefully.

Weapons, Armor, and Items

  • Weapons add attack and may grant critical hits or armor piercing.
  • Armor adds defense and may grant evasion or retaliation.
  • Healing items restore HP.
  • Damage items ignore enemy armor.
  • One ward blocks one enemy strike.
  • Static Smoke weakens the next enemy strike that would actually hit.

Selling or donating equipped gear removes its bonuses. The game warns you before equipped gear is donated to a patrol.

Multiple combat impacts make their sounds one after another. A blocked or evaded strike makes no hit sound.

Experience and Levels

Winning battles awards XP. When the XP meter is filled, your hero gains a level, becomes stronger, gains maximum HP, and is fully healed.

9. Finding Regional Bosses

  1. Visit a village in the region you want to free.
  2. Speak with its residents until someone reports the boss.
  3. Open Inventory → Log → Bosses.
  4. Read the coordinates, distance, and direction.
  5. Turn on that boss's cyan compass if you want guidance.
  6. Reach the fortress tile and defeat the boss.

Only one boss compass may be active at a time. Selecting another boss changes the target. The cyan arrow points in a straight line; it does not find a safe path, avoid water, rent boats, or prevent encounters.

A red compass belongs to an accepted trade or emergency relief delivery. A green compass belongs to an active airship assembly trip. All three colors may appear at the same time.

Defeating a regional boss raises your maximum HP, restores some or all health according to difficulty, halves random encounters in that region, unlocks Reconstruction Boards in that region's villages, and gives its settlements a better chance to recover.

A defeated boss's final words are recorded in the Bosses tab. Its fortress becomes a gray ruined lair.

10. Ruins, Discoveries, and Radio

Ruins

Ruins are searched automatically the first time you enter. They may contain equipment, consumables, travel provisions, or nothing useful. Once searched, a ruin is marked explored and never refills.

Unknown Sites

Thirty unusual locations are hidden throughout the world. Before entry they are labeled Unknown Site. Entering one reveals its name and a short story from the old world.

Discovered sites are kept in Inventory → Log → Discoveries. An airship mission may point to a site that is still unknown. Starting that mission does not reveal the site's name or story.

Radio Towers

Enter a radio tower to switch its transmitter on or off and choose Song 1, Song 2, or Song 3. An active tower broadcasts within 75 straight-line tiles. The nearest active tower determines what you hear when ranges overlap.

Towers remember their switch and song settings. Broadcasts may be heard during land travel, airship flight, village visits, and battle, but not while boating.

Known settlements inside an active signal's range can coordinate exchanges more reliably. The transmitter does not create a reconstruction link, join a confederation, or make an inn free.

11. If You Are Defeated

Reaching zero HP in battle, at sea, or in the air returns you to your registered refuge with full health. You lose some gold, with a larger penalty on harder difficulties.

You keep your levels, XP, equipment, discoveries, defeated bosses, completed reconstruction links, patrols, confederations, trade agreements, settlement history, and grounded airships.

Defeat ends an unfinished relay journey, fails any active commercial or emergency relief delivery, and erases all progress on an unfinished airship assembly. Rest at inns often so your refuge follows your travels.
Failed emergency cargo does not reach its destination. The source settlement's contribution remains spent, the crisis may continue, and no reward is paid.
If you are defeated while flying, the airship currently carrying you is destroyed. Other grounded airships remain where you left them.

12. Rebuilding the World

Reconstruction becomes available in a region as soon as that region's boss is defeated. You do not have to wait until all ten bosses are gone.

Make a Village Connection

  1. Visit a village in a liberated region and open Village → Rebuild.
  2. Choose Start Relay Journey Here. Starting costs nothing.
  3. Travel normally to a different village whose regional boss has also been defeated.
  4. At the second board, donate the displayed gold, provisions, and one consumable.

A basic link does not create fast travel and does not make the road safe. It connects two villages, makes both inns free, and provides a stronger route for supplies and materials than informal regional exchange.

Guard a Link

At either endpoint, donate one weapon and one armor to establish a patrol. The equipment is removed permanently. Better donated gear can widen the patrol later.

A patrol reduces random encounters on land near its connection and makes simulated settlement shipments more reliable. If the regional boss is also defeated, both protections help. Patrols do not protect water, remove fixed boss battles, or move your hero automatically.

The Living Network

Discovered villages continue changing while you travel. They produce and consume supplies, gather materials, maintain security, and build prosperity. Their biome and shops influence their natural strengths, while regional bosses and unsafe routes create pressure.

Villages in the same region may attempt small informal exchanges before reconstruction. These exchanges are limited and unreliable. They do not make inns free and do not count as reconstruction connections.

Formal links carry more aid. Guarded links carry it more safely. Resources can move through several settlements over time, allowing a distant shortage or recovery to spread through a connected network.

Open Inventory → Network after reconstruction becomes available. Overview shows links, patrols, confederations, and agreements. Chronicle shows known settlement conditions, active crises, and major events.

The Chronicle records only important changes, not every small increase or decrease. Village Notes provide the most detailed local report and identify developments since your previous visit.

Settlement Crises

Supplies, Materials, Security, and Prosperity may become strained or dire. If a condition remains dire, the village can enter a crisis. Internal links and confederation aid may resolve it without the Wayfarer.

A crisis never erases the village, closes its shops, disables its inn, or removes its residents. It represents hardship and creates opportunities for recovery.

Confederations

When three or more connected independent villages form one group, they automatically establish a confederation. Smaller independent settlements may later join it.

Confederation members coordinate aid more effectively through their existing links. They remain separate villages with their own local conditions.

Established confederations remain separate governments. Building a connection between two of them does not merge them.

Commercial Trade Deliveries

Separate confederations may establish a free trade agreement from the village Trade tab. They do not need a reconstruction link between them.

  1. Establish an agreement with another confederation.
  2. Accept one displayed delivery offer.
  3. Follow the red compass to the named destination city.
  4. Enter that exact city to deliver the cargo and earn gold.

Successful commercial deliveries provide a small prosperity benefit to their settlements. You may carry one active trade mission at a time. The cargo is kept separate from your normal inventory. Cancelling the mission or being defeated removes the cargo and pays no reward.

Emergency Relief Missions

A dire crisis inside one confederation may request help from a separate confederation that already has a trade agreement with it. Eligible partner Trade boards display the emergency offer before ordinary commercial offers.

  1. Visit a village in the partner confederation that has enough of the needed settlement resource.
  2. Open the Trade tab and accept the emergency relief request.
  3. Follow the red compass to the exact distressed village.
  4. Enter it to deliver the sealed cargo and receive the promised reward.

Emergency cargo is prepared from the source settlement's simulated reserves and remains separate from your personal inventory. The destination receives a substantial Supplies, Materials, Security, or Prosperity increase when the delivery succeeds.

If ordinary network flow resolves the crisis while you are already traveling, the accepted mission remains valid. Your delivery still pays its promised reward and strengthens the village against the crisis returning.

Cancelling an emergency mission or being defeated loses the cargo. The source contribution is not restored, no gold is paid, and the distressed settlement may issue another request after a delay.
Inventory → Log → Trade Missions records what the Wayfarer accepted and delivered. Inventory → Network → Chronicle records what happened to the settlements themselves.

13. Build an Airship!

Villages in Regions 6 through 10 have an Airship Assembly workshop in the Rebuild tab. The workshop is separate from the ordinary Reconstruction Board.

Three rare components are required. Each component must be recovered and returned before the next trip begins.

Complete the Airship!

Trip 1 - Lift Material

Follow the green arrow to the selected Discovery Site. The location may still be an Unknown Site. Entering it reveals the discovery normally and provides the first component.

Trip 2 - Stabilizer

After returning the first component, follow the green arrow to a defeated boss lair. A living boss's fortress cannot supply the part.

Trip 3 - Navigation Coil

After the second return, follow the green arrow to the nearest eligible radio tower. Recovering the component does not switch off, damage, or otherwise change the transmitter.

Finish the Job

Return the third component to the village where construction began. Open the Rebuild tab and choose Complete Airship. The finished craft appears on the nearest empty grass, beach, or desert tile.

The green arrow always points toward the next required destination. After a component is recovered, it points back to the assembly village.
If you are defeated before completion, every returned and carried component is lost. Start a new assembly from the beginning.

You may build as many airships as you like. New assemblies rotate their Discovery Site and defeated-lair targets, and use another eligible radio tower. Several grounded airships may exist at once.

14. Saving Your Adventure

The game saves automatically after important progress changes. Press the on-screen Save button whenever you want to save immediately.

Settlement conditions, active crises, emergency deliveries, and Network Chronicle history are saved with the rest of your world. Loading a save does not reroll completed simulation events.

Saves normally remain inside this browser. Clearing browser storage, changing browsers, or moving to another device can make that local save unavailable.

Export a Save

  1. Press Save while playing.
  2. Reload or reopen the game to return to the title screen.
  3. Select Export Save.
  4. Keep the downloaded JSON file somewhere safe.

Import a Save

  1. Select Import Save on the title screen.
  2. Choose a Scipath Wayfarer JSON save file.
  3. Confirm replacement if a browser save already exists.
  4. Select Continue Saved Game.
A successful import replaces the existing browser save. Export the old save first if you may want it again.

15. Wayfarer's Hints

  • Do not rush into high-level outer regions.
  • Rest at inns before a dangerous journey.
  • Carry healing items and check what equipment is actually worn.
  • Watch a boss or mini boss's next-action warning.
  • Ask several residents when searching for a boss.
  • Read Village Notes when returning to a settlement.
  • Residents' professions affect what local changes they notice.
  • Buy provisions before you need them.
  • Count enough provisions for the return trip.
  • Remember that a compass gives direction, not a safe route.
  • Search ruins when you see them; each can be looted only once.
  • Different villages carry different shops and equipment.
  • A failed retreat gives the enemy a chance to strike.
  • A basic link carries more aid than informal exchange.
  • Guarded links move aid more reliably than basic links.
  • Check the Network Chronicle for active settlement crises.
  • Emergency relief offers appear only at eligible partner boards.
  • Commercial and emergency deliveries share one mission slot.
  • Land your airship before provisions reach zero.
  • You cannot land directly on a village or landmark.
  • An Unknown Site can be an airship component source.
  • Grounded airships survive ordinary battle defeats.

16. Victory

Defeat all ten regional bosses to end the old command and complete the campaign.

Your adventure does not have to end there. You may continue exploring, finding old-world discoveries, operating radio towers, connecting villages, guarding routes, watching settlements grow and recover, forming confederations, answering emergency relief requests, completing commercial deliveries, and building airships for as long as you wish.

Good luck, Wayfarer. The road-and the sky-are waiting!
