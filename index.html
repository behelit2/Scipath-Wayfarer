Instruction Manual

Explore a procedurally generated post-apocalyptic world, help its settlements, defeat ten regional bosses, and rebuild connections between villages as you liberate their regions.

Campaign objective: defeat the boss in each of the ten regions. Defeating a regional boss unlocks Reconstruction Boards in that region's villages immediately. You can build and guard links during the campaign, then continue rebuilding after all ten bosses fall. Reconstruction is optional and has no required network size.

Quick Start

  1. Enter a character name and select a difficulty.
  2. Move using the keyboard or by touching the map.
  3. Fight wilderness enemies to earn gold and experience.
  4. Enter villages and speak with their residents.
  5. Check Inventory → Log for reported boss coordinates. Turn on a known boss's compass to display a directional arrow near the hero.
  6. Buy equipment, healing items, and sea provisions.
  7. Stay at an inn to heal and register a recovery refuge.
  8. Defeat a regional boss to reduce that region's encounters and unlock its village Reconstruction Boards.
  9. Optionally connect villages in liberated regions and donate equipment to guard their links.
  10. Defeat all ten bosses to complete the campaign. Keep exploring and rebuilding afterward if you wish.

Difficulty

Choose Easy, Normal, or Hard when starting a new game. Difficulty affects starting health and gold, enemy attack strength, rewards, character growth, supply prices, resale values, and defeat penalties. Your selected difficulty is retained in the save.

World and Regional Progression

The world contains grasslands, forests, deserts, frozen reaches, coasts, seas, villages, ruins, abandoned radio towers, and fixed regional boss lairs. Region level rises with distance from coordinate (0, 0), up to region level 10.

Outer regions contain much stronger enemies. You may enter them early, but doing so can be extremely dangerous.

Each region has its own boss and reconstruction unlock. Defeating the region 1 boss unlocks boards in region 1; defeating the region 2 boss unlocks boards in region 2; the same rule applies through region 10.

Boss order is not enforced. Defeating a boss out of order unlocks its own region, not every lower region. The campaign ending still requires all ten regional bosses to be defeated.

Regional Boss Log and Compass

Knowledgeable villagers report the coordinates of their region's active boss. These reports are retained in Inventory → Log, together with the source of the report, the approximate distance, and the direction from your current position.

Once an undefeated boss's location is known, a Compass button appears within that boss's entry. The button displays its current state: Compass: Off or Compass: On. Click an off button to enable it, or click the active button again to remove the arrow.

Only one boss compass can be active. Turning on another boss's compass automatically switches guidance to that boss; you do not have to turn the previous one off first.

While enabled, a cyan arrow appears approximately two tiles ahead of the hero in the direction of the selected boss. It points toward that boss's reported coordinates and updates as you move. Its placement is based on the boss's direction, not the direction you last moved. The arrow scales when you change tile size.

Near the target, the arrow can sit closer to the hero. At the exact target coordinates, there is no remaining direction to indicate, so the arrow is hidden. Defeating the targeted boss automatically turns its compass off. Unknown and defeated bosses do not offer compass toggles.

The compass indicates a straight-line direction, not a safe route or an automatically generated path. It does not avoid water, reduce encounters, provide fast travel, or remove boat fees and provision requirements. Plan the journey normally.

Reading the Log or changing a compass target does not consume a battle action. The selected compass target is retained in your save. Older saves without a compass selection begin with it off.

Sea Travel and Provisions

Entering water on foot requires a 10-gold boat rental. Landing leaves the boat behind, so a later sea departure requires another rental.

Every water tile consumes one provision. Once provisions reach zero, each additional water tile removes 5% of maximum HP, rounded upward.

Sea starvation can defeat you. Carry enough provisions for the outward journey and the return trip. Reconstruction links, guards, and the boss compass do not remove boat fees, provision use, or starvation.

Item Shops sell provision packs that cover 200 water tiles. Provisions are added directly to your supply total rather than stored as an inventory item.

Villages, Inns, Ruins, and Radio Towers

Villages contain residents, an inn, and possibly Weapon, Armor, or Item Shops. Not every village has every shop. Knowledgeable residents reveal the coordinates of their region's active boss; those reports are retained in Inventory → Log, where you can also enable compass guidance.

A normal inn stay costs 5 + 4 × region level gold. It restores all HP and registers that village as your recovery refuge. A village that is an endpoint of a completed reconstruction link offers free inn stays.

Merely entering a village does not heal you or change your refuge. You must choose an inn stay, even when the stay is free.

Ruins are searched automatically the first time you enter them. They may contain equipment, consumables, or 100–200 sea provisions, but some contain nothing useful. A searched ruin remains permanently explored and does not refill.

Abandoned Radio Towers appear on land at approximately half the frequency of ruins. Their steel mast symbols distinguish them from other landmarks. Enter a tower to turn its transmitter on or off. Activation is optional.

An active transmitter broadcasts a repeating signal within 75 tiles, measured in a straight line. Leaving the tower does not disable it. Return and choose Turn Transmitter Off to stop that tower's broadcast. Transmitter states are retained in your save.

Active towers have a green beacon and blue broadcast markings. Overlapping broadcast areas play one copy of the signal rather than stacking the audio. Leaving every active broadcast area stops the sound; reentering starts it again.

If your browser blocks signal playback, press a key or tap the game again while within range. Radio broadcasts can be heard during ordinary travel, village visits, and combat.

Combat and Equipment

Attack, use consumables, change equipment, or attempt to flee. Regional boss battles cannot be fled. Mini bosses can be fled, but they have stronger stats and dangerous combat patterns.

Bosses and mini bosses show their next response in the gold telegraph box. Watch for heavy attacks, armor changes, healing, piercing strikes, and multi-strike responses.

  • Critical hits deal 1.75 times normal damage.
  • Armor piercing ignores part of enemy defense.
  • Damage items ignore enemy armor.
  • One ward blocks one complete strike, not an entire multi-strike response.
  • Static Smoke weakens the next strike that would connect; blocking or evading does not consume that weakening.
  • Failed flee attempts trigger the displayed enemy response.
  • Using an item or changing equipment during battle consumes your action.
  • Reading the boss Log or Network ledger, or toggling the boss compass, does not consume an action.

Equipping gear adds its combat benefits while you own and wear it. Selling equipped gear removes those benefits immediately. Donating equipped patrol gear also removes its benefits, but requires a separate confirmation warning.

Experience, Leveling Up, and Sound

Victories award experience. When your XP reaches the requirement shown in the HUD, the hero gains a level. Leveling increases maximum HP according to difficulty, increases base attack, and periodically increases base defense. Your health is fully restored when you level up.

A level-up chime plays whenever an experience award raises the hero's level. If one award grants several levels, the chime plays once rather than layering several copies over one another. Ordinary XP gains and loading a saved game do not play the chime.

The level-up sound uses levelup.wav, stored in the same directory as this HTML file. Keep the filename and capitalization exact. Browser audio restrictions or a missing audio file do not prevent leveling or interrupt gameplay. Interact with the game to allow audio playback if your browser requires a user gesture.

Defeat and Regional Victory

Defeat in battle or at sea returns you to your registered refuge with full HP and costs some gold. Inventory, equipment, XP, boss progress, and remaining provisions are retained.

Defeating a regional boss permanently halves random encounter chance in that region, increases your maximum HP, restores health according to difficulty, and records the boss's final words in Inventory → Log. If your compass was tracking that boss, it turns off automatically.

That victory also unlocks Reconstruction Boards in every village of the defeated boss's region. You do not need to wait until the campaign ends to begin rebuilding.

Defeat cancels an unfinished relay journey. Completed links and their patrols remain.

Reconstruction Network — During the Campaign

Visit a Reconstruction Board in a village whose regional boss has been defeated. Start one relay journey at village A, then travel normally to a different village B and establish the link at B's board.

Both endpoint regions must be liberated. The villages may be in the same region or in different unlocked regions. Visiting a village in a locked region does not complete the journey; defeat that region's boss or continue to another eligible village.

The board lists previously visited villages and shows whether their reconstruction is available or locked. Your destination may also be a village you discover along the way.

Only one relay journey can be pending at a time. Starting it costs nothing. You can cancel it without penalty at an available board.

Establishing a Basic Link

At the destination, the board previews the construction cost:

  • Gold: 20 + 4 × the higher endpoint region level + ⌈straight-line distance / 10⌉
  • Provisions: 8 + ⌈straight-line distance / 8⌉
  • One consumable selected from your inventory.

The ceiling symbol ⌈ ⌉ means round upward to the next whole number. Construction resources are charged only when you establish the link. Travel expenses, including boat rentals and sea provisions, are additional.

The donated consumable is permanently removed without applying its healing or battle effect. If resources are missing, the journey remains pending while you resupply.

A basic link appears as a gold connection line on the map and makes inns free at both endpoints. A basic link does not reduce encounter chance by itself.

Guarding and Widening a Link

At either endpoint, choose one weapon and one armor, then use the Guard Link button beneath the selection controls. The equipment is permanently donated to establish a patrol.

A guarded link appears as a green connection line. Its protected land corridor halves random encounter chance. Where the regional boss is also defeated, the two reductions multiply, leaving one quarter of the original encounter chance. Overlapping patrol corridors do not stack.

The protected radius around the connection is: 1 + min(2, floor((weapon attack + armor defense) / 14)) tiles. The maximum radius is three tiles.

Better gear can widen an existing patrol using Widen Patrol. Equal or narrower replacements are refused, and previously donated equipment is not returned.

Donating equipped gear requires confirmation and lowers your attack and/or defense when the gear is removed. Patrols protect land within their corridors only; they do not prevent fixed boss battles, create roads, provide fast travel, or make water safe. Ordinary travel risks still apply.

Tracking Your Work

After your first regional boss victory, open Inventory → Network to view established links, guarded links, towns connected, the largest connected network, and any pending relay journey.

The Network ledger is separate from the boss Log. Reconstruction adds no extra bosses or mandatory network quota. Existing links and patrols remain after campaign completion, and rebuilding can continue indefinitely.

Saving and Continuing

The game saves automatically after progress changes. Use Save to save immediately, then choose Continue Saved Game on the start screen when you return. Your selected boss compass target is saved along with your other progress.

Saves are stored in this browser. Starting a new game replaces the existing save after confirmation; Delete Save removes it. Clearing browser storage or opening the game in another browser can make the saved game unavailable.
